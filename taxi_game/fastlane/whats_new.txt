What's new:

- The first screen is unmistakably Cab Hustle now, and it states the
  rules before you drive: three crashes end an Endless Shift, and
  every dropoff is a bank-or-push call.
- The Daily Shift is one shared course for everyone, and your own
  ghost is waiting to race you after your run.
- The menu shows your next goal, the next cab or the next
  achievement, and every run summary carries it too.
- Shift stats are one tap from the menu now, not tucked behind
  Settings.
- The garage says what its bars change, so buying a cab is a
  driving decision.
- The steering stick switches thumbs instantly, and its ring fades
  out when you lift your thumb.
