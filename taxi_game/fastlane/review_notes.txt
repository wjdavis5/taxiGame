Cab Hustle is an independently developed arcade game, written from scratch
in Dart for Flutter and the Flame engine. Everything in the app is original
to this project: the code, the top-down sprite art drawn for this game, and
the audio (CC0-licensed packs listed in the app's bundled license
inventory, plus sounds synthesized in this repository). It is built on no
third-party app template and shares no assets or code with any other app.

It shares a genre - one-thumb driving - with other games, so here is what
makes it its own game, visible from the first session:

- Daily Shift: one course per day, the same for every player, with a ghost
  of your best attempt to race; the once-a-day attempt and its result are
  saved on-device.
- Endless Shift: a run with three-strike lives that only ends when a third
  crash forfeits whatever is unbanked; at every dropoff you bank the fare
  chain or push on for more.
- A near-miss economy: shaving past traffic pays, so precision driving -
  not distance - is what scores.
- Vehicles differ on real handling axes (top speed, acceleration,
  steering, body size), unlocked through a garage progression, with
  records, lifetime stats, and achievements.

There is no account, no network traffic, no ads, no in-app purchases, and
no tracking; all progress lives on the device. We are glad to answer
anything else App Review needs.
