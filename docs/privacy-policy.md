# Privacy Policy for Cab Hustle

**Last updated:** 23 August 2026

## The short version

Cab Hustle collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, and no network connection of any kind. Nothing you do in the app
is transmitted anywhere, because the app cannot transmit anything.

## What the app stores

Cab Hustle saves your game progress — the level you have reached and your coin
total — to your device's local storage. That data:

- stays on your device,
- is never sent to us or to anyone else,
- is not readable by us,
- is deleted when you delete the app.

There is no other data. No name, no email address, no device identifier, no
location, no contacts, no photos, no usage statistics, no crash reports.

## What the app does not do

- No user accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics or telemetry of any kind.
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No third-party tracking SDKs.
- No network requests. The app functions entirely offline.
- No in-app purchases.

## Children

Cab Hustle is safe for all ages with respect to data. Because the app collects
no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.
This is consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
and equivalent regulations.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects or transmits anything, this policy will
be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here.
