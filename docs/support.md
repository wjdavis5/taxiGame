## Common questions

**How do I play?**
Tap Play from the main menu. Steer the taxi by touch, pick up your passenger at
the marked pickup point, and deliver them to the drop-off marker without
colliding with traffic. Completing a level earns coins and unlocks the next one.

**Is my progress saved?**
Yes. Your level and coin total are saved on your device automatically. They are
never uploaded anywhere — see the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

**I lost my progress after reinstalling.**
Progress is stored locally on the device only, so deleting the app deletes the
save. There is no cloud backup in this version.

**Does the game work offline?**
Always. Cab Hustle makes no network requests at all.

**Does the game have sound?**
Not in this version. Audio is planned for a future release.

**Which devices are supported?**
iPhone and iPad running iOS 13 or later. The game is portrait-only.
